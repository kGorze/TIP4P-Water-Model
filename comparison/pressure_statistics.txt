Note about Pressure Fluctuations in NPT Simulations:
------------------------------------------------
Large pressure fluctuations are normal in NPT simulations, especially for small systems.
The fluctuations scale as 1/√N, where N is the number of particles.
Only the average pressure should converge to the target pressure (0.987 atm).
Standard deviations of hundreds of atm are typical for water systems.
