Note about RMSD Interpretation:
-----------------------------
RMSD measures structural deviation from a reference structure (first frame).
Lower RMSD values indicate structures closer to the reference.
Equilibration is detected when RMSD fluctuations stabilize.
For water systems, RMSD primarily reflects diffusion and reorganization of water molecules.
Differences between systems may indicate varying degrees of structural stability or mobility.
